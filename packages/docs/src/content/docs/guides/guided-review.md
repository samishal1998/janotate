---
title: Guided review
---

Open **Guide** in the review sidebar and choose **Start guide**. The **Full review** route starts with the first item and visits its parts in order. **Browse stops** groups the outline by item. Pick a stop to open its exact location, or use **Next stop** and the back arrow.

- On a decision, the guide shows which option is recommended or selected. On a plan, it shows the selected stage’s status. Rankings, ranges, defaults and scope cards show their current values. Changing an answer in the item updates the guide immediately. A **draft** label stays visible until you save the answer.
- On a diagram, choosing a walkthrough step opens the matching variant and caption. Use **Play** or **Pause** in the guide or on the diagram. Both control the same playback, and the guide follows each step. On an animation, named Lottie markers open their frame or range.
- On a diff, schema, function overview or PR review, choose a hunk, column, code block, change group or finding to inspect it in context. Use the item’s discussion panel to comment there.

Choose **Plan stages**, **Proposal** or **Sections** for a route through plan and proposal content. Choose **Threads** to visit discussions and see their reply counts. Visiting a stop leaves the answer for you to choose.

**Float** detaches the guide. Drag its handle, or focus **Move guide** and use the arrow keys. **Home** returns it to the sidebar. **Collapse guide** keeps your current stop while you work.
