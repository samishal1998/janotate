title: Reviews and items
---
A review groups questions that one developer can answer together. Each item carries a title, evidence and a source. The source records who supplied the value and whether it was stated, inferred or unverified.

An asked item needs a ruling. A defaulted item still needs explicit confirmation. Choosing a recommendation confirms it. A different choice corrects it. Saving an answer makes the draft visible to the agent while you review other items. Submitting commits the review. A skipped item counts as neither approval nor rejection.

## Anchors and threads

Use the guide to visit individual options, stages, diagram steps and discussions. [Guided review](../../guides/guided-review/)

In a proposal, the comment icon at the upper right of a section opens its discussion composer. You can also select a passage to comment on its exact wording.

Choose **Annotate** in the review toolbar to use the comment cursor. Click a decision option, diagram node or schema column to open its comment box. In a proposal, click a section or drag across a passage to quote it. Touch users can tap a target. Press Escape or choose **Annotate** again to return to the default tool.

Select text inside an item to retain the quote with its surrounding context. Use a named comment button to identify a whole option, diagram node, edge, block or hunk. Add a comment to save it. Replies retain the anchor and previous revision. HTML forms support whole-item comments.

## Decision log

Choose **Decision log** on the home page to search prior rulings, or call decisions_query before opening a review. The home page also lists the six most recently active open reviews. A later ruling with the same question supersedes the earlier log entry. Historical review results stay intact. The exported Markdown files are under the data folder in decisions/. Commit the files you intend to share. Session reviews stay out of this log.

## Ending a review

**Submit review** sends the final result to the agent. To finish without sending a result, open **More review actions** beside Submit and choose **End without submitting…**. Janotate asks for confirmation. The agent receives a cancelled status. Saved answers and comments remain visible in the review, and unsaved edits are discarded.
